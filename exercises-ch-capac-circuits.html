    Method A - Equivalent Capacitance Method

    3. Hybrids of parallel and series connections.

    To find the equivalent capacitance of a hybrid of parallel and series connections, the items need to be simplified in the correct order. This means finding the smallest sections of purely parallel or purely series combinations, replacing those sections with equivalent capacitors, then repeating the process until all capacitors are replaced by a single capacitor.
    🔗
    Sometimes, it’s hard to tell which sections to simplify first, if some sections are nested inside other sections. To make it easier to tell which sections are inside other sections, it can help if you first locate all the nodes (places where wires split or join) in the correct order.
    🔗
