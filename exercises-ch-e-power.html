At about 4 am you decide to cook some breakfast. Despite being sleepy you’ve got the coffee perking. Now you want to make some waffles but you realize there might be a problem. You are trying to figure out whether the electrical outlets are connected together in series or parallel when your friend reminds you that when you disconnect the coffeepot, the waffle iron stays on.
